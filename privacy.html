Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-18 · नेपालीमा पढ्नुहोस्

English

What you can do without giving us anything

Finding drivers, calling or messaging them, and calling emergency numbers need no login and send nothing about you to Ghoom.

When you log in with Google

Google handles the login; we never see your password. Ghoom receives your name, email address and a Google account ID, and stores them with the role the Ghoom team gave you (user, driver or admin). Admins can see this list of people so they can give roles.

When you register a driver

The driver's name, phone number, vehicle type, vehicle number, usual place and language, a map pin of that place if you choose to save one, the driver's consent, and your Google email and account ID as the person who sent it. Admins see this to check the driver, usually by calling them. If approved, the driver's details (not yours) go on the public list.

Reports and feedback

If you report a listing, we keep the driver concerned and the reason. If you send feedback, we keep your stars, your words and the driver it is about. Neither includes your name, email or number.

What stays on your phone

If you allow location, it is used only to show how far you are from drivers' usual places, and it never leaves your phone. A map pin is read once, when you tap the button. We do not track where you go. Your settings, favourite drivers, the drivers you called last, notifications and any app-lock PIN (stored scrambled) stay on your phone. The number of people you choose for a ride goes only into the message your phone sends to the driver.

What others can see

An approved driver's name, phone number, vehicle type, vehicle number and usual place, including any map pin, are visible to everyone using the app, along with whether they said they aren't driving today. That is what lets passengers find and call them.

Where it is stored

Accounts, roles, driver requests, reports and feedback are stored with Google Firebase, which runs our database and login and may store data outside Nepal. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long, and your choices

We keep your details while your account or listing exists, and a driver request until an admin approves or rejects it. You can delete your account in Settings > Delete my account. This phone forgets you at once, a driver's listing is hidden, and we delete the rest within 7 days after checking it was you. You can also ask us to correct your details. Logging out only signs you out of this phone.

Children

Ghoom is not meant for children under 16 to log in or register drivers. A driver must meet the legal age to drive.

Changes

If what we do with your data changes, we will update this policy and the date above.
